Respected Madam,

At the outset, we place on record our admiration for the Department's sustained work in child protection and welfare across Maharashtra, and for your personal commitment to strengthening the State's child-protection machinery.

We write to seek a brief appointment at your convenience to demonstrate **Project Khozo**, a working platform developed by the **Aegis School of Data Science & AI, Mumbai**, under our *AI for Social Good* initiative. Khozo has been built to address a specific and painful operational gap: the delay and fragmentation that occurs between the moment a child goes missing and the moment the right officer, Child Welfare Committee or institution is able to act on a credible sighting.

**What Khozo does.** Khozo is a single, role-based platform that connects citizens, NGOs, the Police and the child-protection ecosystem in one auditable workflow — *report → verify → match → refer → restore*. A parent, citizen or NGO can report a missing child or upload a photograph of a child they have spotted; the system ranks possible matches against registered cases, routes the sighting to the jurisdictionally correct desk, and escalates to Childline 1098 / the Child Welfare Committee where no match exists. Every action is recorded in a tamper-evident audit trail.

**Grounded in the existing Government framework.** Khozo has not been designed in isolation. Its workflows, stakeholder roles and data model were developed after a structured study of the Government's own child-protection systems — **Mission Vatsalya, TrackChild, KhoyaPaya, the GHAR portal (NCPCR)** and **CARINGS** — and of the statutory processes under the **Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015**. Accordingly, the platform natively supports the roles your Department works with every day: **CWC, DCPU, SJPU, AHTU, DLSA, CCIs, JJBs, RPF, DCRB/SCRB, SARA and SAA**, alongside the State Nodal Officer. It records Social Investigation Reports and Individual Care Plans, tracks 24-hour production compliance before the CWC/JJB, maintains CCI admission and care registers, and follows restoration and repatriation through to formal closure. Cases can be linked to existing TrackChild, KhoyaPaya, CCTNS/FIR, NCRB and GHAR identifiers, so that Khozo **complements the Government's systems rather than duplicating them**.

**Child privacy has been treated as a design constraint, not an afterthought.** Public-facing screens are redacted by default — guardian contacts, identity numbers, exact addresses and protected photographs are never exposed. Photograph upload requires recorded consent, purpose and a retention period, and a supervised privacy-review queue governs retention, extension and anonymisation.

**What we would like to present.** With your permission, we would be glad to conduct a **live demonstration of approximately 30 to 40 minutes**, covering the citizen reporting journey, the Police and CWC/DCPU review workflow, the district and State command dashboard with its MIS report, and the privacy and audit safeguards. We would value, above all, the Department's guidance on where such a system would be most useful on the ground.

**We wish to be candid about the platform's present status.** Khozo today is a fully functional, end-to-end demonstrable prototype, developed and tested by our faculty and student teams. It is offered to the Government in the spirit of public service, and our request is for the Department's direction on a **supervised pilot in one district**, so that the system may be validated against real operational conditions, integrated with the Government's authorised face-recognition and identity systems, and hardened for production before any wider deployment.

We shall be happy to present at Mantralaya or at any location and date convenient to your good office, and can make ourselves available at short notice. Should the Department prefer, we would equally welcome the opportunity to first demonstrate the platform to the Principal Secretary (WCD), the State Nodal Officer, or the State Child Protection Society.

We are deeply grateful for your consideration, and we hope for the privilege of an opportunity to present this work before you.
